What runs it

Both pieces run on the Devuan laptop, and both are already in the design.

  • Zigbee2MQTT talks to the dongle over USB. It is the "hub" the plug listing asks for, with its own web page for pairing, naming and settings.
  • Mosquitto carries state and watts out and on/off commands in. The shedder reads and writes those messages.

Not needed: Home Assistant, a ThirdReality hub, the ThirdReality app, or any account.

Work that is not written yet:

  • Zigbee2MQTT needs Node.js, and its install guide assumes systemd. Devuan needs a small sysvinit start script.
  • Put the dongle on a short USB extension cable, away from USB 3 ports, which interfere with Zigbee.

Why these

The September research ranked three families for an outage with no internet:

  1. Zigbee plugs with a USB coordinator. No Wi-Fi, no cloud, no internet clock. Chosen.
  2. Z-Wave (Zooz ZEN15 for motor loads, ZEN04 for lamps). Best range, $30 to $45 a plug. The alternative if Zigbee disappoints.
  3. Pre-flashed ESPHome or Tasmota (Athom US 16 A). Fully local, but on Wi-Fi, and UL listing unverified.

Not TP-Link for new plugs. TP-Link support confirmed that a Tapo P110 loses energy monitoring after a power loss until it sees the internet again, and a firmware update can break local control. The HS110 you own is the old protocol and stays.

Gen2 over Gen3. Both are in Zigbee2MQTT with the same metering and both expose the power-on behaviour setting, so the Gen3's "power-on restoration" is not exclusive to it. The Gen2 has seven times the reviews.

ZBDongle-P over -E. Current guides call the P the safer pick for Zigbee2MQTT. Not checked against Zigbee2MQTT's own adapter page.

Dehumidifier

Your observations of 2026-10-10, not measured by an agent:

  • It resumes by itself after a power loss, so a plug on restores it.
  • It holds its compressor for 5 minutes at start-up when the humidity is above the set-point. That covers the Powerwall's transfer gap; no delay protector needed.
  • At start-up it runs only the fan, about 75 W. The relay never closes onto the compressor, so the plug's motor rating is not a concern for this unit.

The shedder's 3-minute minimum-off rule is now redundant for this unit and stays in the design as cover for a later one.

Decided the same day:

  • Model: Electrolux FFAD5033W10.
  • Below the set-point the fan still runs, at 60 to 85 W for 3 minutes, then the unit idles at 2.3 W.
  • Restore check: the shedder reads the plug 1 minute after on; above 40 W means the unit is running. At 40 W or less it reads again at 2 minutes and alerts only if that is low too. Compressor draw is not checked.
  • The shedder logs each metering plug's watts to a file on the laptop about once a minute. A Zigbee plug never appears in the Kasa app.
  • Power-on behaviour previous on every Zigbee plug.

To do when you pick this up

  1. Buy the two items from "Saved for later". Recheck price, seller and the title.
  2. Read the label on a plug: 15 A and the safety listing.
  3. Install Mosquitto and Zigbee2MQTT on the Devuan laptop; pair one plug.
  4. Set each plug's power-on behaviour to previous, so a transfer gap does not undo a shed. Decided, untested on hardware.
  5. Test with the internet blocked at the router: pull the plug's power, then confirm on/off and the watts reading both work after it boots.
  6. Do not update plug firmware once this passes.
